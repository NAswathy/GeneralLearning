Summary

Lean Business Case / Problem Statement

AreaDetails
Epic Statement For Thermo Fisher customers and internal support users, the TF ECom site must provide a reliable and consistent digital commerce experience from product discovery through purchase and post-purchase tracking. The solution should connect product data, entitlement, pricing, availability, cart, checkout, quote, and order history capabilities so users can evaluate products and complete transactions with confidence.
Business Outcome Hypothesis By standardizing the end-to-end TF ECom journey on existing platform services and clear integration contracts, Thermo Fisher can improve product discoverability, reduce purchase friction, increase add-to-cart and checkout completion, improve support deflection, and create better visibility into commerce performance.
Leading Indicators Search-to-PDP rate, PDP engagement, price and availability success rate, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, quote submission, order history visibility, error/fallback rate, and support path usage.
Business Analysis Summary The TF ECom site depends on coordinated behavior across identity, catalog, search, PDP, pricing, availability, cart, checkout, payment/order submission, post-purchase, analytics, and support. The MVP should reuse existing patterns wherever possible and limit custom development to confirmed gaps.
CAS / Support Considerations Support teams need visibility into user context, country, login state, selected SKU, price action, availability result, cart state, checkout errors, quote ID, order ID, and failed service responses.

Scope

In-Scope

  • Customer identity, login, registration, and account-context handling for supported TF ECom journeys.
  • Country, language, cookie, and user-context rules that affect catalog, pricing, availability, and checkout behavior.
  • Catalog data ingestion and product visibility through Hybris/catalog, Platform API, and search index flows.
  • Search and product listing behavior, including product cards, filters, sort, entitlement, and no-result handling.
  • PDP and Magellan product detail behavior, including product content, specifications, images, lifecycle messaging, package selection, pricing, availability, and Add to Cart eligibility.
  • Cart behavior for selected SKUs, quantity, price, availability, line-item messages, promotions, and validation.
  • Checkout behavior for customer details, shipping, billing, quote handoff, order submission, and integration with downstream order systems.
  • Post-purchase experience including order confirmation, order history, order status, quote/order references, and customer service visibility.
  • Analytics and monitoring for the full commerce funnel and critical service failure paths.
  • Release readiness, test planning, operational handoff, and support playbook alignment.

Out-of-Scope

  • Full ERP replacement or migration work outside the commerce capability boundary.
  • Manual correction of product records outside approved catalog governance processes.
  • New payment, tax, or order-management platform selection unless separately approved.
  • New regional dealer portal capabilities unless explicitly added as a TF ECom feature.
  • Advanced personalization or recommendation algorithms beyond existing platform capabilities for MVP.

Acceptance Criteria

Feature 1: Product Discovery and PDP

IDAcceptance Criteria
AC-01Search returns only products visible for the selected country, customer/account context, and entitlement rules.
AC-02Search results display product identity, image, key attributes, lifecycle/status indicators, and supported commerce action cues.
AC-03PDP displays product title, catalog number, package options, specifications, images, content sections, approved notices, and lifecycle messaging where data exists.
AC-04Changing package or SKU refreshes price, availability, banner, product documents, Add to Cart state, and analytics context.
AC-05If product content, image, pricing, availability, or related service data fails, the page remains usable and shows a safe fallback state.

Feature 2: Commerce, Cart, Checkout, and Post Purchase

IDAcceptance Criteria
AC-06Pricing and availability are resolved by selected SKU, quantity, country, account, entitlement, and login state.
AC-07Add to Cart is enabled only for orderable products and disabled for invalid, restricted, long-term unavailable, or unsupported purchase states.
AC-08Cart recalculates totals, validates quantity, displays SKU-level messages, and prevents checkout when blocking issues exist.
AC-09Checkout validates required customer, shipping, billing, quote, and order-submission data before final submit.
AC-10Order confirmation and post-purchase views show order reference, status, submitted items, quote references where applicable, and support path.

Feature 3: Analytics, Operations, and Support

IDAcceptance Criteria
AC-11Analytics events are emitted for search, PDP, package change, price, availability, Add to Cart, cart update, checkout step, order submit, quote, error, and support interactions.
AC-12Monitoring dashboards include service success/error rates, latency, fallback usage, checkout errors, and order submission outcomes.
AC-13Support users can identify user context, selected SKU, country, account, price/availability result, cart state, checkout error, quote ID, and order ID where available.
AC-14Release readiness includes product, architecture, analytics, UX, support, CIS, privacy, and compliance sign-off.

Supplementary Information

Analytics and Reporting

  • Required funnel events: search, result click, PDP view, package change, price lookup, availability lookup, Add to Cart, cart update, checkout step, order submit, quote submit, order confirmation, support contact, and error/fallback states.
  • Required dimensions: country, language, login state, account type, SKU, package, product family, ERP/system context, price action, availability status, cart state, checkout step, and error code.
  • Dashboards should show funnel conversion, service errors, latency, fallback usage, checkout abandonment, order submission outcomes, and support path utilization.
